# GPIMS — Government Procurement and Inventory Management System

Documentation-first Laravel procurement and inventory application based on RA 12009 and applicable implementing regulations.

**Status:** Laravel 13 + Filament 5 scaffold (phase 0) and identity/organization module (phase 1) shared services with master data (phase 2), planning with budget allotments (phase 3), and the supplier registry with compliance (phase 5) implemented. Phase 4 (purchase requests) is next. Full schema (tables, models, factories) for phases 2–10 is in place; workflows and screens for those phases are next. See [docs/20-PHASE-1-STATUS.md](docs/20-PHASE-1-STATUS.md) and [docs/21-SCHEMA-IMPLEMENTATION.md](docs/21-SCHEMA-IMPLEMENTATION.md).

See the `docs/` directory for legal traceability, requirements, workflows, architecture, roadmap, testing, and deployment planning.

> Planning materials are not legal certification. Verify the effective IRR, current GPPB issuances and applicable COA/DBM/DICT requirements before enabling compliance rules.
