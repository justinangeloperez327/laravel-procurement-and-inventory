# 15 — Implementation Roadmap

| Phase | Indicative duration | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 Foundation | 1 week | Laravel bootstrap, PostgreSQL, CI, docs |
| 1 Identity/organization | 2 weeks | Roles, policies, delegation, audit |
| 2 Planning/budget | 3 weeks | Scoping, PPMP, APP, allocations |
| 3 PR/suppliers | 3 weeks | Requests, approvals, supplier eligibility |
| 4 Procurement/bidding | 5 weeks | Modes, RFQ/bids, BAC, evaluation |
| 5 Contracts/award | 3 weeks | Award, NOA, NTP, PO and securities |
| 6 Receiving/inspection | 2 weeks | Receipts, IAR, partial acceptance |
| 7 Inventory/property | 4 weeks | Warehouse stock ledger and assets |
| 8 Finance/compliance | 3 weeks | Invoice matching, disputes, sanctions |
| 9 Reports/integrations | 3 weeks | PMR, forms, authorized adapters |
| 10 Testing/deployment | 3 weeks | UAT, security, deployment and recovery |

Total illustrative sequential estimate: **32 weeks**. These are not contractual delivery commitments.

## MVP
Single agency, departments, supplier/item masters, PPMP/APP, budget, PR, key procurement modes, RFQ/bid, BAC award, contracts/PO, receiving/inspection, stock ledger, basic assets, invoices and audit reports.

## Definition of done
Approved requirements, legal traceability where relevant, implementation, passing tests, role checks, documented migrations, acceptance and updated operational guides.
