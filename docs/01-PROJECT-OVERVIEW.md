# 01 — Project Overview

## Product
Government Procurement and Inventory Management System (GPIMS). Laravel-based, intended for one Philippine procuring entity with multiple departments and warehouses.

## Objectives
- Trace every procurement from market scoping, PPMP and APP through purchase request, award, contract, receipt, inspection, inventory/property and invoice tracking.
- Preserve authorized decisions, approvals, document versions, financial movements and audit evidence.
- Apply effective-dated procurement rules under RA 12009 and other applicable regulations, with accountable human decisions.

## Scope
Organization and RBAC, supplier registry, catalog, planning, budgets, purchase requisitions, procurement modes and bidding, BAC, contracts, inspection, warehouses, assets, invoice matching, compliance and reports.

Not initially in scope: replacement of government accounting or PhilGEPS; external e-bid submission absent approved integration; legal compliance certification.

## User roles
HoPE, planning, budget and procurement officers, BAC and TWG, department requesters and heads, inspection/receiving, supply/property, accounting, auditor, administrator and future supplier portal.

## Assumptions
Agency-specific approval delegations, procurement forms, funding/account codes, integration permissions and official IRR applicability require validation before production use.
