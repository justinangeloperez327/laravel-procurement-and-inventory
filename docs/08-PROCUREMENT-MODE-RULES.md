# 08 — Procurement Mode Rules

RA 12009 Article IV recognizes Competitive Bidding, Limited Source Bidding, Competitive Dialogue, Unsolicited Offer with Bid Matching, Direct Contracting, Direct Acquisition, Repeat Order, Small Value Procurement, Negotiated Procurement, Direct Sales, and Direct Procurement for Science, Technology and Innovation.

The application should screen candidate modes, capture justification and require appropriate authority. Criteria depend on category, agency type, ABC, CSE sourcing, exclusivity, timing, previous award, emergency basis and current IRR.

Rule schema: `rule_code`, `mode`, `legal_reference`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `entity_type`, `category`, `conditions`, `exceptions`, `approved_interpretation`, `reviewer`, `test_reference`.

Example tests: repeat order limits and original-contract linkage; direct contracting evidence; procurement splitting review; RFQ minimum solicitation requirements; transitional legacy-law applicability.

**Do not hardcode proposed peso ceilings.** Validate current GPPB resolutions, updates, interpretation and date of applicability before rule activation.
